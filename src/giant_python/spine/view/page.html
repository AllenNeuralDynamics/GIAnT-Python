What is shown
  • Size: each source's readout of a per-trial image in its fixed window (the mean footprint over the pixels with a value in every trial), normalized as spineTrends.m: log, minus each trial's median over the sources, minus the source's own mean; shown as % against that mean.
  • Step: the largest t of a change between the trials before and after a split (after trial: the last trial before it); brief: the strongest change of 1–3 trials that comes back, against the 2 trials on each side; beyond trend: the step test once the source's part of the shared trend (first singular vector over the sources) is removed. p from random trial orders of the source, q from Benjamini–Hochberg per path; bold where q ≤ the FDR level.
  • Field: each trial divided by its median (bleach-normalized), or their mean. Change: trials blurred 0.7 px, against the mean of all trials or the previous trial in the same block of consecutive acquisitions; at the mean, the last block against the earlier ones (one block: second half against first); brightness follows the mean image. Activity: the run's activity image as a robust z.
  • Crops: the window (footprint weights; contours at 50% and 10% of the peak; hatched: pixels left out because some trial has no value there), then the mean and every trial, on one contrast.
  • Marks belong to a position and attach to the nearest source within 2.5 px, in any product of the session.
