What the panels show
  • Activity image (Sources view): SILo's combined activity image of the path on its consensus canvas (all trials), or one trial's, as in experiment_summary.h5 and per_trial_summary.h5. Markers sit at the kept sources' footprint centroids, the kinetics-rejected sources' pixels and, when the run kept its plan, the seeds whose pixel got no source.
  • Aligned movie (Movie view): the trial's registered 80 Hz movie (*_REGISTERED_DOWNSAMPLED-80Hz, activity channel) in the trial's own registered frame, not resampled. Blank (NaN) at SILo's motion discards and at frames whose kernel weight lies mostly on SILo's discarded 200 Hz frames; only pixels missing in less than nanThresh of the frames are shown.
  • Fitted activity: every kept source's footprint times its denoised trace (dF_denoised), rendered in the movie's pixels and frames as the F0 network's training-data exporter does. Time: each 80 Hz frame averages SILo's 200 Hz traces at its frame lines with weights exp(−|Δline| / dt) within max(3 dt, P) lines (P: lines per cycle), leaving out SILo's discarded frames. With the exporter's kernel dt is the movie's own (ceil(mean(diff(DSframes))), 134 lines on these rigs), as the training-data exporter does; but the 200 Hz frames are themselves averages (dt200 = 54 lines), so this smooths fast events twice and keeps 93–95% of an event's 80 Hz peak on noiseless injected events. The matched kernel uses √(dt² − dt200²) (≈ 123 lines) and keeps 97–99%. The Time kernel menu switches between them. Space: the trial's footprints are moved from the consensus canvas into the trial's frame through the trial's across-trial map (rigid, affine or patchwise), bilinearly; the exporter instead searched a rigid offset per trial by correlation and blurred the footprints to match the movie's shape (here a fixed blur, 0 unless the server was started with --blur). Kinetics-rejected sources are not included (their traces are not in the summary).
  • Residual = aligned movie − fitted activity: what a training target made from this run would be. Residual − baseline = movie − activity − the F0 network's baseline (only with --baseline-dir).
  • Units: raw counts of the registered movie (each pixel's weighted mean of its raw SLAP2 samples); SILo's traces and F0 are in the same units (footprints sum to 1, so a trace is the source's total over its footprint).
